03 / PROGRESSION

Make participation count.

ONYX uses global leveling, so a member's progression follows their ONYX profile rather than restarting separately in every server.

GLOBAL LEVELS

Progress across servers

Members earn XP and advance through the leveling system as they participate in communities using ONYX.

PRESTIGE I–X

Milestones worth earning

Reach Level 60 to advance through Prestige. Each Prestige milestone awards 10 Prestige Tokens.

PRESTIGE MASTER

Long-term titles

After Prestige X, continue through Prestige Master levels and unlock profile title milestones up to level 1,000.
