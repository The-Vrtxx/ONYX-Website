Privacy
Policy.

An overview of information that may be processed when you use ONYX.

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Service: ONYX Discord bot and onyxdiscord.com
Developer / Operator: Vrtx
Project: ONYX Development
Contact: ONYX Support Discord ↗

Important transparency note

This policy describes categories of data the bot's features may need. The operator must verify the live database schema, hosting logs, backups, third-party processors, retention periods, and deletion workflow before treating this as a final legal policy. No unverified retention duration or provider is claimed here.

1. Scope

This Policy covers information processed by ONYX in connection with the bot and its website. Discord and hosting providers may separately process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Information That May Be Processed

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs needed to perform requested bot functions.
  • Progression information: XP, levels, Prestige progress, leaderboard data, profile settings, token balances, and cosmetic ownership or selections.
  • Server settings: Configured channels, roles, welcome settings, moderation settings, logging destinations, ticket settings, and other feature configuration.
  • Moderation and safety records: Warnings, moderation cases, staff notes, security events, and report or ticket content where those modules are used.
  • Command and diagnostic information: Information included in command interactions or error reports that is needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the Service.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To respond to commands and operate enabled bot features.
  • To maintain leveling, Prestige, profiles, role settings, moderation records, tickets, and backups when those features are enabled.
  • To secure, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To respond to support requests and investigate abuse or technical problems.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by Discord and by infrastructure or hosting providers used to operate the bot or website. The exact providers and their processing locations must be confirmed by the operator and reflected here before public launch. ONYX does not claim that it sells personal information.

5. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate the relevant features, maintain security, resolve issues, and meet applicable obligations. Specific retention periods, backup lifetimes, and deletion timing have not been independently confirmed for this deployment and are therefore not stated as fixed durations. A request to review or delete information can be made through the ONYX Support Discord. The operator will need enough information to identify the relevant Discord account or server and may need to verify the request.

6. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect stored information and credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Never send bot tokens, passwords, database credentials, or private environment variables to support staff.

7. Children and Age Requirements

ONYX is provided through Discord. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and comply with applicable age-related rules in their location. The Service is not designed to collect information directly from children below the applicable minimum age.

8. Your Choices

You can stop interacting with ONYX, remove it from a server, or contact the project through the support server with a privacy request. Removing the bot does not necessarily immediately erase previously stored records or backups; deletion requests should be submitted separately.

9. Changes

This Policy may be updated as features, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Vrtx through the ONYX Support Discord. Please avoid posting private details in public channels; ask staff for an appropriate private contact route.
